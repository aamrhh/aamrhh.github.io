About me

I am a final-year Bachelor of Computer Science student at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), specialising in Big Data. I previously graduated with a Diploma in Computer Science, where I received the Vice-Chancellor's Award for making the Dean's List in every semester.

I have always been drawn to the "why" behind things. When I see a number or a trend, I want to understand why it looks that way and what is driving it. Data analytics gives me a structured way to follow that interest, turning raw data into evidence that explains patterns and supports better decisions. Beyond finding the cause, I also enjoy working out what can be done about it, and I am building the knowledge and experience to propose solutions that are grounded in data.

Through academic and personal projects, I have worked across the full analytics workflow, from collecting and cleaning data to modelling and presenting results. I have used Python, SQL and Power BI to analyse national blood donation trends, measure how oil prices affect the ringgit, and build machine learning models for text and classification tasks.

I am still early in my journey, but I learn quickly and genuinely enjoy picking up new tools and methods. Each project has taught me something practical, from being patient with messy data to explaining technical findings in a way people can act on. I am now eager to gain industry experience that will strengthen my skills in data science and analytics.

Projects

Final Year Project: Telco Sentiment Analysis

In progress

Developing an NLP-based aspect-based sentiment analysis (ABSA) system to evaluate the service quality of Malaysian telecommunication providers (Maxis, CelcomDigi and U Mobile), using user reviews from the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. The project aims to build a machine learning model that better understands mixed-language Malaysian text, where English and Malay often appear in the same review.

The system covers aspect category detection (ACD), which identifies what each review is about, such as service reliability, customer service or app performance, followed by sentiment classification for each aspect. I planned the full end-to-end pipeline, from collecting and preprocessing reviews to modelling, evaluation and a results dashboard.

  • Python
  • Web scraping
  • NLP
  • Aspect category detection
  • Machine learning

Code will be shared after the project is complete.

How Oil Prices Affect the Malaysian Ringgit (2005–2026)

Live

Analysed 20 years of crude oil prices and USD/MYR exchange rate data to measure how changes in oil prices affect the value of the Malaysian ringgit, using correlation and regression analysis in Python.

Built and deployed an interactive Streamlit dashboard that walks through the analysis step by step, from data preparation to the final results. The dashboard updates automatically with the latest market data on weekdays. Markets are closed on weekends, so no new values are added then.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • Correlation and regression
  • Streamlit
